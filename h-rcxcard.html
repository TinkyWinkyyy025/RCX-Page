For our investor partners

Investor Partnership
Principles

投资人合作原则

Thank you for your continued trust and support. To help our partnership run smoothly, we'd like to share a few guiding principles:

感谢各位一直以来对我们的信任与支持。为了让彼此的合作更加顺畅愉快,谨与各位分享几项核心原则:

01 — TRUST & DIRECTION

Trust the professionals

Day-to-day operations, staffing, and management decisions are entrusted to the management team. We'd be grateful for your continued trust and the space to carry these out.

专业分工,彼此信任

日常营运、人事与管理决策,交由管理团队全权负责,恳请各位股东给予充分的信任与空间。

Speak openly, work it out privately

Your feedback and ideas are always welcome. Should any disagreement come up, we'd love to work through it together, privately.

坦诚交流,私下商量

我们非常欢迎各位随时提出宝贵意见;若有不同看法,建议以私下沟通的方式共同商量,寻求最佳共识。

Lead with data, not emotion

We’d encourage reviewing financial reports regularly and keeping sight of long-term goals, especially through short-term ups and downs.

理性决策,着眼长远

建议定期查阅财务报告,掌握营运状况,并以长远目标为重,与团队一起从容应对短期的起伏。

02 — SUPPORT & CONDUCT

Lend a hand, share in the upside

If you're able to lend your expertise or networks to help promote the business, we'd truly appreciate it.

携手助力,共创双赢

若各位愿意运用自身的经验与人脉,协助宣传推广、引荐顾客,我们将非常感激。

Settle up on the spot, on the same day

For smooth bookkeeping, we kindly ask that personal spending at the venue be settled on the spot, on the same day (cash, card, or transfer all work).

现场消费,当日结清

为方便账务管理,敬请各位股东与宾客在现场消费时当日结清(现金、刷卡或转账皆可)。

Respect the team

We’d ask that intoxicated or disorderly conduct be avoided, and that all staff be treated with respect. Verbal or physical abuse toward staff will not be tolerated.

尊重团队,以礼相待

敬请各位股东与宾客避免醉酒失态,并请以礼相待全体员工;任何针对员工的言语或肢体冲突、骚扰行为,我们都无法接受。

03 — STANDARDS & STEWARDSHIP

No outside food or drinks

To help us maintain quality and hygiene standards, we'd ask that outside food or beverages not be brought onto the premises.

谢绝外带饮食

为维护场内品质与卫生标准,敬请各位与宾客避免携带外部食品或饮料入场。

Stay compliant, together

We're committed to keeping operations compliant with all relevant laws, and would appreciate your support in upholding this.

依法合规,携手前行

我们会确保营运符合相关法律法规,也恳请各位股东一同支持合规经营。

Protect what we've built

We'd ask that all business-related information stay strictly confidential, so we can protect what we're building together.

用心保密,共同守护

感谢各位股东对商业相关资讯予以保密,一起守护我们共同经营的成果。

Here's to a wonderful partnership ahead!
期待与各位携手同行,共创佳绩

The important details

Terms &
Conditions.

Participating outlets, discounts, benefits and terms may be updated from time to time.

01 — SHOW YOUR CARD

Card eligibility

  • Present the Shareholder Privilege Card before payment.
  • For registered cardholders only. Non-transferable.
02 — WHERE IT APPLIES

Using your benefit

  • Applicable for dine-in and takeaway.
  • Not applicable on promotions, discounted items or merchandise.
03 — EXCLUDED ITEMS

What is not included

  • Alcoholic items at Rama V and Thai Hou Sek outlets are excluded.
  • Cigarettes and other excluded products are not covered.
04 — SPECIAL DAYS

Holiday reminders

  • Not applicable on the eve of public holidays.
  • Also excluded on Father’s Day, Mother’s Day and Valentine’s Day at Restaurants & Cafes Division outlets.
