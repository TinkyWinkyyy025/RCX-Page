The brand

After dark, above the ordinary.

Taking Yangon’s party scene up a notch, The Attic is a place to see and be seen after the sun sets on Myanmar’s cosmopolitan city. An extension of The Beer Factory, an award-winning bar, The Attic bears all the hallmarks of the brand’s trademark industrial-themed interior. Yet, with its kaleidoscope of UV black lights, glow-in-the-dark wall art and lasers taking centre stage, The Attic maintains an edgy individuality that is second to none in its class.

Amidst this setting, party-goers can partake in The Attic’s pulsating energy brought on by the latest tracks spun out by local and international DJs every weekend. Combined with a fully equipped bar that offers some of the choicest liquors, cocktails and beers, The Attic promises to deliver trendsetters and revellers a raving good time, every night of the week.
