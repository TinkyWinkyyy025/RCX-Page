The brand

Craft beer. Good food. Great company.

Tap Room KL is a unique dining and entertainment destination that brings together the best of craft beer and delicious food. The venue is home to Malaysia’s own craft beer brand, PAPERKITE, and offers a wide variety of modern contemporary inspired flavors of tapas to complement the beer. With a seating capacity of 500, the venue is perfect for both small and large groups.

One of the highlights of Tap Room KL is the Pizza Duo, which serves great Neapolitan pizzas with a modern twist, straight out of our custom-built Stefano Ferrara oven. These pizzas are made with only the freshest and highest-quality ingredients, and are cooked to perfection in the wood-fired oven.

In addition to the delicious food and craft beer, Tap Room KL also features a live stage where we showcase some of the best home-grown artists and musicians. This provides a unique and interactive dining experience, as diners can groove along to the live performances while enjoying their food and drinks.

Overall, Tap Room KL is a one-of-a-kind microbrewery experience centre that offers a unique combination of delicious food, craft beer, and live entertainment. It’s a perfect destination for a night out with friends, a casual date, or a special occasion. With a wide range of options available, there’s something for everyone at Tap Room KL.
