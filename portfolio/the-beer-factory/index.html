The brand

An industrial spirit. A place to gather.

The Beer Factory™ is one of the most talked about drinking place in Malaysia. Spearheading the scene with its industrial factory-themed bar and conceptualized designs across individual outlets, The Beer Factory™ boasts a wide selection of beers imported from around the world.

To date, The Beer Factory™ has served over 3 million litres of beer to customers and has been an award-winning bar for over half a decade including the Hospitality Asia Platinum Awards (HAPA), Heineken Global Championship and Stella Artois World Draft Master Regional Final.
