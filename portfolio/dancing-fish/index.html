The brand

Malay-Indo cuisine, reimagined.

Dancing Fish is an award-winning contemporary Malay-Indo restaurant that serves up authentic traditional dishes. With their ethos of offering traditional Malay-Indo delicacies with flair, Dancing Fish’s dishes are a fusion of old and new, combining age-old recipes and fresh modern techniques to bring a whole new dimension of flavour to traditional cooking methods. Since its establishment in 2009, the restaurant has been lauded with award after award for its outstanding food and environment.

Its unique food concept offers a contemporary twist to traditional village dishes. From starters to mains, Dancing Fish brings traditional recipes to life using modern techniques. The restaurant’s signature dishes are cooked from scratch on a daily basis to preserve the homemade flavour – a refreshing change from mass food production. The menu consists of a delicious selection of wholesome meats, fresh vegetables and moreish rice dishes with each plate crafted with care and quality ingredients.

Dancing Fish’s unpretentious yet stylish ambience, coupled with its delicious dishes in generous portions, offer diners an outstanding dining experience. Dancing Fish’s creative approach to local staples, as well as their commitment to using top quality raw ingredients cooked in traditional methods, has not only proven itself the talk of foodies in town but also among tourists who yearn to taste an authentic slice of Malaysia. Indeed, this commitment to quality has not gone unrecognised; Dancing Fish has been named a MICHELIN Selected restaurant from 2023-2026.
