The brand

A place for every appetite.

Common Space is a mini food hall that brings together a curated selection of top food and beverage (F&B) brands under one roof. It offers a diverse range of dining options, from breakfast to dinner, and everything in between. Customers can enjoy a variety of cuisines all in one convenient location.

The food hall features a mix of established and up-and-coming F&B brands, each offering their own unique menu and atmosphere. The space is designed to be welcoming and comfortable, with plenty of seating options, and a lively atmosphere that encourages socializing and community building.

Common Space is open from sunrise till sunset, making it the perfect destination for breakfast, lunch, dinner, and even late-night snacking. Customers can start their day with a fresh cup of coffee and a pastry, enjoy a leisurely lunch with friends, and end the day with a delicious dinner and a glass of wine.

In addition to the dining options, the mini food hall also features a variety of drink options including beer, wine, cocktails, and non-alcoholic beverages. The atmosphere is perfect for casual dining, and there’s something for everyone, whether you’re looking for a quick bite or a leisurely meal.
