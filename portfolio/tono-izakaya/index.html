The brand

An authentic taste of izakaya culture.

Discover Tono Izakaya Singapore, a vibrant Japanese dining experience nestled in the heart of Marina Bay Financial Centre. Known for its authentic Japanese izakaya atmosphere, Tono Izakaya specializes in expertly grilled yakitori, sashimi, and Japanese-style small plates. Guests are invited to unwind with their signature dishes, including the popular Unagi Don and Engawa Don, crafted with fresh ingredients and grilled over binchotan charcoal for a rich, smoky flavor.

Whether it’s a casual dinner or an after-work gathering, Tono Izakaya offers a cozy, bustling ambiance ideal for both relaxation and enjoyment. Pair your meal with a selection from their curated sake list to fully immerse in the true izakaya spirit, perfect for winding down after a day in the CBD.
