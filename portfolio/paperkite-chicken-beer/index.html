The brand

Chicken done right. Beer with character.

Paperkite Chicken & Beer is all about one thing: chicken done right. From wings to tenders and everything in between, it all leads back to the star of our menu — the CB Original Recipe Chicken. Crafted with a secret blend of handpicked herbs and spices, it’s crunchy on the outside, juicy on the inside, and the reason people keep coming back for more.

Pair that mouthwatering chicken with freshly brewed, award-winning craft beers from Malaysia’s own Paperkite Brewing Co. Brewed to pair with our bold flavours, each pint is crafted with care, character, and zero shortcuts. Together, they deliver the kind of food-and-beer combo that keeps the vibe going — a standout experience for anyone who loves great chicken, great beer, and a place that doesn’t hold back.
