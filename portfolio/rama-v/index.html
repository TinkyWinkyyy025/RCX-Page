The brand

A timeless Thai sanctuary.

A hidden sanctuary in the heart of Kuala Lumpur, Rama V has been redefining Thai dining since 1995. Set within a charming stand-alone bungalow, it offers an oasis of calm where authentic Thai flavours meet modern elegance.

From the elegant main dining hall to private chalets and the serene Fa-Ying Garden, every space is thoughtfully designed for intimate occasions — from romantic dinners and corporate gatherings to weddings.

Guided by a philosophy of affordable luxury, Rama V combines timeless Thai traditions with contemporary touches, delivering warm hospitality and culinary excellence that have made it a beloved destination for three decades.
