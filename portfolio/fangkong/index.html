The brand

Live music. Room to let go.

Fang Kong is a live music bar in Kota Damansara with an indoor capacity of 700 pax and an outdoor capacity of 150 pax, all fitted in a whopping 13,000 square foot area, spanning two floors with a terrace and balcony. Fang Kong was founded and named after a Cantonese phrase that translates to “spacing out”. We know for a fact that anyone who wants to space out after a hectic week at work. One would definitely want a place where they can enjoy some live music, be it acoustic or a tribute band, whilst gulping down bucket of beers with your buddies. Fang Kong is exactly that place.

Our menu? We’ve got comfort food and we’ve got Asian food. That is not all; you can also enjoy Spanish tapas, Western Food, Thai, Chinese and Malay food. We’re sure that you will have a ball of a time hanging out at Fang Kong.

Our first-class sound system provides the best audio for live performances and most importantly, our strong live music lineup for the whole week. Every Monday to Thursday starting 9:30pm and Friday-Sunday starting from 6pm, we have acoustic sessions at balcony, and tribute bands downstairs.
